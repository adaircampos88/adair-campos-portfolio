## Linkage confidence

- **Verified:** the submission receipt or equivalent record names the exact CV.
- **Probable:** a confirmed application and company-specific artifact or exact strategy align, but the submitted filename is absent.
- **Unknown:** the submitted file cannot be established. Unknown variants are excluded from all performance comparisons.

Probable variants remain descriptive only. They should not be counted as evidence that a Product-, UX/UI-, craft-, research-, product-, or domain-led positioning converted or failed.

## New working rule

When Adair selects a role, the application directory must contain:

1. `application_strategy.md` with official proof, hard gates, a requirements matrix, application thesis, honest gaps, and planned changes.
2. `tailoring_manifest.json` naming the exact two-page PDF, its checksum, Product or UX/UI family, Craft/Product/Research/Domain emphasis, seniority posture, claim audit, and approval status.
3. `Adair_Campos_<Company>_<RoleFamily>_CV.pdf`, rendered as two A4 pages with selectable text, working links, complete headings, and no clipping or overlap.

The starting Product and UX/UI CVs are factual foundations, not restrictive templates. Both have now passed page-by-page QA with complete page-two headers. Routine bespoke work remains capped at 45 minutes. Uploading to private Waypoint, activating a material, applying, or contacting anyone still requires separate approval.
