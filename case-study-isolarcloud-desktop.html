Context and responsibility

A workflow where the interface has to explain the system—not merely display it.

Users need to understand current performance, investigate historical changes and distinguish missing data from a real equipment issue. The design challenge is to make that depth scannable without hiding the evidence experienced users need.

01 · USER TASK

Understand status quickly

Recognise whether generation, consumption, storage and device connectivity are behaving as expected.

02 · DESIGN WORK

Structure complexity

Translate technical data into navigation, hierarchy, comparison patterns, visual states and reusable interface components.

03 · COLLABORATION

Work within constraints

Discuss requirements and feasibility with Product, Engineering, Design and Service stakeholders across Europe and China.

One complete workflow

From “Is everything okay?” to a specific next action.

The flow keeps the user oriented as they move from summary to evidence, then to a device or event that needs attention.

Scan

Start with a clear system status, current power values and the most important exception.

Compare

Move from today’s overview to a selectable time range without losing context.

Investigate

Inspect energy curves and device contribution using consistent labels and units.

Diagnose

Separate delayed data, missing data and a genuine device event.

Act

End with a clear route to the relevant device detail, event record or support step.

Alternatives and rationale

Three choices that turn a dashboard into a usable workflow.

These are representative decision patterns from the recreated exercise. They show the trade-offs considered without disclosing confidential product details.

HIERARCHY

Exception first, totals second

A dense wall of metrics supports comparison but slows diagnosis. The chosen direction leads with system status, then the values needed to confirm it.

INTERACTION

Progressive detail

Showing every device and event at once increases scanning cost. Summary cards link to focused evidence while preserving the selected site and time range.

DATA VISUALISATION

Labels plus colour

Colour alone is compact but fragile. Persistent legends, units and direct labels improve accessibility and reduce ambiguity across multiple energy series.
